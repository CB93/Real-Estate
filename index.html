Overview

A spacious home, a mortgage helper, and room to grow

Live it. Rent it. Hold it. Develop it.

Rarely does an opportunity like this come along. Set on an expansive 13,500 sq. ft. lot on a quiet street, this well-kept property gives homeowners, investors, and developers real flexibility.

The main home offers roughly 2,000 sq. ft. of comfortable rancher-style living with 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, plus a large double garage with room for two full-size vehicles.

Adding real value is the approximately 1,100 sq. ft. two-bedroom unauthorized suite with its own level-entry access and private driveway — ideal as a mortgage helper, in-law accommodation, or extended-family space. Five bathrooms and two laundry areas in total.

Outside there is an abundance of space to use: covered and uncovered patios, upper and lower driveways, a substantial 650 sq. ft. shop, and a further 200 sq. ft. storage room. Plenty of room for vehicles, hobbies, equipment, or a home-based business.

Properties combining this much land, living space, mortgage-helper potential, and outbuildings are increasingly hard to find.

A fantastic opportunity for a family, investor, or developer looking for flexibility today and potential tomorrow.

Development Potential

Hold it today, develop it tomorrow

The property sits in an area currently being considered and zoned for duplex development with basement suites — an excellent opportunity for future redevelopment.

In the meantime you can live in it, rent the suite, and hold the land until you're ready to build.
