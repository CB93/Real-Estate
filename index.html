Overview

Spacious Rancher with Mortgage Helper & Development Potential on a 13,500 Sq. Ft. Lot

Rarely does an opportunity like this come along! Situated on an expansive 13,500 sq. ft. lot, this well-maintained property offers exceptional flexibility for homeowners, investors, and developers alike.

The main home features approximately 2,000 sq. ft. of comfortable rancher-style living, with 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, plus a large double garage with room for two full-size vehicles.

Adding significant value is the approximately 1,100 sq. ft. two-bedroom unauthorized suite, featuring its own level-entry access and private driveway. This versatile space is ideal as a mortgage helper, in-law accommodation, or extended-family suite. The property offers a total of 5 bathrooms and 2 laundry areas.

Outside, there is an abundance of space to enjoy and utilize, including covered and uncovered patios, upper and lower driveways, a substantial approximately 650 sq. ft. shop, and an additional 200 sq. ft. storage room. There is plenty of room for vehicles, hobbies, equipment, storage, or a potential home-based business.

Development potential is a major bonus. The property is currently in an area being considered/zoned for duplex development with basement suites, presenting an excellent opportunity for future redevelopment while allowing you to live in, rent, and hold the property until you're ready to develop.

Located on a quiet street with southern exposure, close to an elementary school and SkyTrain, this is a convenient and desirable location with excellent long-term potential.
